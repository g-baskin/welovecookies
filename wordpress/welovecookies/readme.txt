Cookie consent that follows the law where each visitor is.

== Description ==

Shows each visitor the cookie banner their location calls for: ask-first in the EU, UK, Brazil and other opt-in countries, a short notice with "Do Not Sell or Share" in US privacy-law states, and nothing intrusive where no consent is needed. Analytics and ad tags stay blocked until allowed, and Global Privacy Control is honoured.

== Installation ==

1. Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, choose welovecookies-wordpress.zip, then Install Now and Activate.
2. Set your privacy policy page under Settings > Privacy. The banner links to it automatically.
3. Optional: add the [welovecookies_settings] shortcode to your footer so visitors can change their choice.

Developers can change any setting with the `welovecookies_config` filter.

This is engineering guidance, not legal advice.
